hush

Local AI chat for Apple Vision Pro.

A local LLM in your headset. No cloud, no account, no recurring fee.

The model produces every response without leaving your device after first launch. There is no server, no telemetry, and no account. Your conversations stay on the headset.

What you get

  • Multi-turn conversations with streaming responses.
  • Optional voice input. Tap the mic, speak, tap again to submit.
  • Optional voice output. Responses are read aloud and spatially placed at the chat window.
  • Two themes. PAPER (warm cream + saddle brown) or TERM (near-black + amber).
  • Thinking mode reveals the model’s reasoning above each response.

What you don’t get

  • Web search.
  • Plugins or external tools.
  • A subscription.
  • A login screen.
  • Your conversations leaving the device.

One-time purchase

$9.99 on the App Store. Updates included. The model downloads on first launch (~2.5 GB) and then runs entirely offline.

System requirements

  • Apple Vision Pro running visionOS 26 or later.
  • ~3 GB free storage for the model.
  • Internet connection only for the first-launch model download.
