What Hush collects

Nothing.

Hush does not have a server. There is no account to create, no login, and no telemetry. Every conversation you have with the model happens on your Apple Vision Pro and never leaves your device.

The only data Hush stores locally on your device is:

  • Your conversation history (turns of user input + model responses), persisted via Apple’s SwiftData framework. Stored exclusively in the app’s container; never transmitted anywhere.
  • Your settings (chosen theme, voice output toggle, thinking-mode toggle), persisted via Apple’s UserDefaults. Never transmitted anywhere.

Tapping “Reset conversation” in the app’s Settings sheet deletes all stored conversation history immediately and permanently.

What Hush transmits

Exactly one network request, and only on first launch:

  • A model-weights download from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) for the on-device LLM. This is a one-time download of approximately 2.5 GB. After this download completes, Hush makes no further network requests for the lifetime of the app.

Hugging Face’s privacy policy governs that single download: huggingface.co/privacy.

What Hush does not do

  • No analytics. No tracking. No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No remote logging or crash reporting.
  • No background activity.
  • No cloud sync of conversations or settings.

Voice input and voice output

When you use the optional voice input feature, your speech is processed by Apple’s on-device speech recognition. The audio and resulting transcript never leave your device. When you use the optional voice output feature, Apple’s on-device text-to-speech synthesizes the audio locally.

Children’s privacy

Hush has no age-gated features and does not knowingly target users under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
